# A$500,000 / 17-week DCA after BTC crosses below the 200WMA
**Protocol locked before viewing its real historical outcomes — 12 November 2026. RESEARCH ONLY.**

## Question and source-claim caution

The user supplied an estimate that BTC stayed beneath its 200-week moving
average ~14 weeks in 2018/19, ~8 in the 2020 pandemic, and ~30 in 2022/23,
giving an arithmetic average near 17 weeks. **Those durations are not yet
validated on one consistent weekly-close methodology.** In particular a
multi-week price decline, a daily dip below WMA, and consecutive *Sunday
closes* below WMA are different events. Verify those episode lengths with
real reconstructed Sunday BTC/USD closes, rather than assume 17 is a
reliable parameter.

## Locked strategies

1. **17 calendar weeks, unrestricted full principal**: triggered by first
   Sunday close below 200WMA, A$500k / 17 each subsequent Monday, even if
   BTC subsequently rallies above the moving average; **breaks weekly cap**.
2. **17 calendar weeks, capped**: same calendar dates and attempted sizing
   but cap purchases at A$25,500; leaves surplus cash uninvested.
3. **20 calendar weeks, capped**: equal A$25k weekly for 20 calendar weeks,
   thereby investing all A$500k without violating the weekly limit.
4. **17 qualifying weeks ONLY below 200WMA, unrestricted**: buy A$500k/17
   on at most 17 next Mondays whose prior Sunday is at or below WMA; if
   BTC rebounds quickly, retain cash, do not force deployment.
5. **20 qualifying weeks below 200WMA, capped**: buy A$500k/20
   only while below WMA; missing weeks postpone purchases, possibly
   beyond the 104-week scoring window.
6. **Hold AUD cash**: exposure/interest reference.
7–10. Original **V5.9 risk-gated 1x**, **3x**, **strict 3x at risk<=0.20**,
   and **3x only when below 200WMA**. These start on exactly the same
   first crossing as the fixed 17-week policy, using the same original
   104-week remaining-cash funding formula and A$25,500 cap. Their
   interest income may also be subsequently invested as under PR #259.

### Interpretation / limits

- **Full 17-week / 20-week calendar buying after an early WMA touch often
  wins on BTC units in these rising historical windows**, because it
  aggressively puts a much larger share of cash to work EARLIER.
  The result does **not** independently prove 200WMA bottom-picking
  skill or an optimal 17-week horizon.
- **Exclusively buying under WMA** can fail badly when price recovers
  quickly (2020, and first near 2018). Conversely the August 2022
  30-week strictly-below period allowed the capped 20-eligible-week plan
  to invest its full budget at attractive weekly references.
- The 2022 June and August triggers, and 2023 recrosses, share
  subsequent price paths; they are not independent cycles. 2018 and
  2020 may exhibit extremely different behaviour. Sample size
  remains small and the 17-week rule was proposed after seeing
  historical crashes.
- Real BTC historical USD and lagged AUD FX proxies, not actual
  executable ASX:IBIT prices; past V5.9 source vintages are
  reconstructed and unverified, so this is historical research.
  No cash/AAA taxes or live IBIT tracking/spread guarantee.
- No changes to Production V5.8.2, Research V5.9 live recommendations,
  Cycle Trading, ntfy, Stake or portfolio data. This study is added
  to the existing scheduled **weekly BTC research-only** workflow.
