# 200-week moving-average BTC entry test — 12 November 2026

**Research-only, pre-results protocol.** No change to Production V5.8.2,
Research V5.9 live recommendations, Cycle Trading, NTfy, Streamlit, portfolio
records, or broker orders.

## Question

For an investor holding roughly **41% BTC / 59% AUD cash**, does buying new
BTC only near the **BTC/USD 200-week moving average** improve total-portfolio
returns or acquire more BTC versus (i) risk-gated 1x, (ii) risk-gated 3x and
(iii) strict risk<=0.20 3x? Can the benefit be distinguished from leaving
cash uninvested and collecting 5% interest?

## Predeclared competing bands

All ratios are BTC/USD **prior Sunday** divided by a 200-week mean of
**Sunday-close BTC/USD** (not 200 daily candles). A qualifying buy must also
have reconstructed, actual-source R2 risk <=0.50 (except the stricter
<=0.20 combination); sizing follows the existing aggressive policy
3x/2x/1.5x/1x according to risk, with <=5% initial capital per week.
A 3x fixed-size within-10% band sensitivity is included separately.

- **Below 200WMA:** ratio 0–1.00.
- **Below to 5%, 10%, or 20% above:** ratio 0–1.05 / 1.10 / 1.20.
- **Within 5%, 10%, or 20% of the mean:** ratio 0.95–1.05 / 0.90–1.10 / 0.80–1.20.
- **10% below only:** ratio 0–0.90.
- **Below through 10% above, plus risk<=0.20:** ratio 0–1.10, strict threshold.
- **Within 10%, fixed 3x**, only at R2 risk<=0.50, cost and weekly caps unchanged.

The distinction matters: a strict symmetrical proximity band **excludes**
a still-cheaper price more than 5% or 10% below the average; "below to +10%"
does not. Exact 200WMA levels are inherently lagging and not guarantee of
cycle bottoms.

Record median final total portfolio value, worst holdout, win cohorts,

average purchase price**, and price selection versus spreading each
strategy's **same eventual net purchase dollars** across all 104 weeks.
That latter comparator is **hindsight-only diagnostic**, not a deployable

time or holding cash. Preserve every per-cohort outcome in the artifact.

### Validity limits

All six holdout cohorts share market weeks and are not independent BTC
cycles. Retrospective reconstruction does not verify original provider
publication vintages; historical settings / selection can overfit.
Historical BTC/AUD spot is not an actually executable ASX:IBIT quote.
A 5% cash return is assumed pretax, not a promise of fixed AAA yield.
One-time costs and ETF drag are illustrative. No recommendation for
immediate buy/sell or live promotion follows from these comparisons.

**Interpretation:** Restricting buys to the 200WMA improves **BTC
purchase-price selection**, e.g. at/below the mean had a median
+41.0%
additional BTC units on a same-net-spend equal-week comparison,
but the calendar gate permitted only
15
median buy weeks in a 104-week period and left substantial AUD cash
not exposed to the subsequent rise. Median total-portfolio value
was **-5.47%**
versus strict risk<=0.20 3x. This is a concrete case where good
*prices on executed trades* do not automatically translate into the
best *portfolio equity*. Underinvestment often reduces drawdowns.

A narrow symmetrical +/-10% proximity gate is particularly poor in
this retrospective study because it excludes good prices **more than**
10% below the average; for a buy-only investor the one-sided
"at/below +10%" gate is logically more coherent, though it also lagged
strict <=0.20 on median portfolio value.

### Conclusion

**Do not switch to an exclusive 200WMA-only buy policy.** Existing
risk<=0.20 aggressive DCA remains a *research candidate*, not proven
optimal, while existing Production V5.8.2 and live V5.9 recommendation
must remain unchanged. An at/below +10% 200WMA gate is a legitimate
secondary valuation indicator for research. Further prospective testing
must compare actual date-stamped ETF executable prices, true cash
interest distributions, and how frequently the user remains
uninvested after missed 200WMA touches. No future 2027 prices were
invented.
