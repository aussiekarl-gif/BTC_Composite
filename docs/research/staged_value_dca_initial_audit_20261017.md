# Staged Value/Recovery DCA — Initial Research Audit (17 Oct 2026)

**Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Do not deploy to live Cycle Trading / ntfy / brokerage or SMSF portfolio.**

## Goal, method and limitations

Test larger, staggered DCA buys in substantial 365-day drawdowns even when BTC remains above its strictly prior 200WMA. Existing live default is BUY WATCH only when previous BTC close is below the prior 200WMA, 2.5% of remaining cash. Challenger tiers: 3–20% of remaining cash with prior-day 200WMA, 365-day drawdown and improving prior 50DMA checks.

The 25%-above-cycle-bottom central VWAP error and 20% stretch are **post-hoc targets**, not strategy inputs. Each requires >=50% deployment. Any unfinished cycle has **no final pass/fail verdict**.

Prices: archived Bitcoin/USD daily chart exports through **5 October 2026**. Both strategies start with hypothetical **US$500,000 uninvested cash** on the same **halving +620-day** date, buy no more often than every 7 days, and use completed observations strictly *before* the execution-day close. Use identical US$3/fill costs and retain at least 2% initial cash. At the same **halving +1150-day** horizon, evaluate cash invested, BTC purchased, ex-post window-low weighted VWAP error, and terminal BTC-equivalent **including remaining cash**. 2017/2021 complete historic windows; 2025/26 **RIGHT-CENSORED**. 2013 intentionally excluded.

No AUD FX, SMSF capital-gains tax, prior Bitcoin inventory, executed sales, future cash deposits, IBIT spreads/tracking, trading slippage, or live portfolio balances. These are **not** after-tax SMSF portfolio figures. End-of-day close is not a guaranteed executable fill. All historical cycles and threshold ideas have already been inspected; this is **not out-of-sample validation**.
